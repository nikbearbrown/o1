# Criterion 3: Published material about the person

## Executive summary

**The criterion.** Published material about the person and their work, in professional or major trade publications or major media.

**Why this folder exists.** So agents can look for opportunities for a person to contribute in this one area, and keep the evidence tidy as it appears. A petition needs evidence for at least three of the eight criteria, and the officer then weighs the whole record against the real standard (sustained acclaim, top of the field). This is one of the eight. Nothing in this folder is legal advice; the attorneys decide what goes in a petition.

**State.** Empty. No opportunities have been searched for and no evidence has been logged yet. `opportunities.csv` and `evidence-log.csv` are headers only.

## What counts

Articles that substantially discuss the person's work. Must include title, date, and author, and the outlet's audience or circulation should be documented.

## What USCIS discounts

Passing mentions or brief citations. University news-office stories are self-generated and weak. Paid placements are a red flag. The outlet must qualify as professional, major trade, or major media. Earned coverage takes months.

## What an agent can do

1. Search for existing coverage of the person and their work and save an archived copy of each, with title, date, author, outlet and the outlet's audience or circulation figures.
2. Classify each item: substantial discussion, passing mention, self-generated, paid. Only the first counts at full weight.
3. List professional and trade outlets and journalists who cover the person's field and a plausible story angle for each. Draft pitches for a person to edit and send.
4. Check that a story is about the person and their work, not a topic story that mentions them.

## What only a person can do

- Send pitches and speak to journalists.
- Decide what to say publicly about work, other people and unpublished results.

## Evidence to keep

- Archived copy of the article
- Title, date, author, outlet
- Audience or circulation evidence for the outlet
- Note on whether it is earned, self-generated or paid

## Files

- `opportunities.csv`: one row per opportunity found. Columns: `found_on`, `opportunity`, `type`, `url`, `deadline`, `fit_note`, `status` (open, pursuing, done, dropped), `decided_by`, `evidence_ref`.
- `evidence-log.csv`: one row per piece of evidence, logged when it is created. Columns: `date`, `item`, `proof_file`, `source_url`, `counts_for` (this criterion, or another, or step 2 only), `note`.

See `../AGENTS.md` for the rules every agent follows in this folder (no fabrication, nothing sent or submitted without a person's yes, record every source).
