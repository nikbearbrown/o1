# Tier-Concentration and Occupational Employment Resilience, 2018-2024

## Executive summary

**About.** An observational test of whether occupations concentrated in the upper tiers of the seven-tier taxonomy held employment better over 2018-2024 than Tier-1 occupations, using about 800 BLS OEWS occupations, with a 2012-2018 placebo period and AIOE as a control. It uses correlational language only and names causal identification as future work.

**Status.** Rough draft, 794 words, 16 open TO DO markers. No results. The tier-concentration score cannot be computed until Paper 1's rubric is settled.

**Blocks.** Depends entirely on Paper 1. Two motivating claims (programmer decline starting 2001, graphic designer employment flat) are marked as unverified against BLS series.

**Next.** Finish Paper 1's rubric, then run the BLS pull and the placebo test.
