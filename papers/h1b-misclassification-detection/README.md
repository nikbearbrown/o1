# Detecting Occupational and Wage Level Misclassification in H-1B Filings Using LLM-Based Job Listing Classification

## Executive summary

**About.** Employers self-report both the SOC code and the DOL wage level on H-1B Labor Condition Applications, and DOL does not verify either at certification. The paper classifies the matching job listing independently and measures how often it diverges from the filed values, including downward wage-level mismatch.

**Status.** Draft shell, 3,978 words, 58 open TO DO markers; every rate in the abstract is a blank slot. No results.

**Blocks.** Needs full listing text matched to certified LCA records (the matching is the hard part and is not shown as done), and the classifier from `../llm-soc-classification-validation/`. A paper that says employers misreport carries a high bar for evidence and for how mismatches are worded.

**Next.** Build and audit the match set; hand-check a sample of mismatches before reporting any rate.
