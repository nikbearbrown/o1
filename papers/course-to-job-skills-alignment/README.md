# From Syllabus to Vacancy: Faculty-Verified Course Skill Assertions Against Job-Posting Demand

## Executive summary

**About.** A pre-specified study: can a program state, with evidence and with each claim reviewed by the teaching faculty member, which job-posting skill requirements its courses address and which they do not? It keeps three kinds of evidence apart: a span of syllabus text, a faculty confirmation, and a span of ad text classified as required, preferred, responsibility or mention.

**Status.** Registered-report-style draft, 2026-10-24, 1,275 words. No study has been run. Six local courses and 19 source-linked skill proposals exist in seis.jobs, none faculty-confirmed.

**Blocks.** Authorship, faculty reviewers and whether IRB review is needed are all undecided. The posting collector stores ad hashes, not text, so required/preferred classification needs the text re-read.

**Next.** Decide the IRB question and recruit faculty reviewers before collecting anything.
