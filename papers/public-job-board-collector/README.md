# What a Public Job-Board Collector Can and Cannot See of the Market for Software Engineering and Information Systems Graduates

## Executive summary

**About.** A data-and-measurement paper on the ai-jobs collector: how it finds employers with a readable public job board, reads every posting into one canonical shape, and sorts titles into families tied to SEIS courses, and how much of the market that actually covers.

**Status.** Draft 0.1, 2026-10-24, 2,105 words, with real numbers from one day of collection (2026-10-24): 2,574 companies listed, 719 (27.9%) with a confirmed readable board on one of five ATS systems, 715 boards read, 84,282 open postings, 41% of the 59,449 postings outside one outlier employer matching a SEIS title family, 1,891 entry-level or internship postings by title (1,079 in the US).

**Biggest open problem (the draft's own finding).** The title list was extended using unmatched titles from the same collection it is scored on, so the 41% is partly in-sample. It needs a hand-labelled holdout before it is quoted. One day of data also cannot show turnover.

**Next.** Hand-label a holdout sample; add more collection days; then rewrite with the held-out rate.
