# A Seven-Tier Taxonomy of Human Ability for Classifying AI Capability Exposure

## Executive summary

**About.** A framework paper: seven tiers of human ability (pattern and association, embodied, social and personal, metacognitive, causal and counterfactual, collective, wisdom), each tier boundary taken from an older comparative-cognition literature that has nothing to do with AI. It adds a rule for assigning the 52 O*NET abilities to tiers, and a finding that the upper tiers have no O*NET counterpart at all.
