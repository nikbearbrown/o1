# Has AI Really Transformed the Job Market? Employment, Cognitive Demand, and the Misidentification of AI Risk

## Executive summary

**About.** A working paper arguing that AI-driven employment change tracks the cognitive tier mix of an occupation, not its industry or label: Tier-1 pattern work declining after 2022, social work stable, metacognitive and causal work growing with a wage premium. It uses 2012-2024 BLS OEWS data against O*NET 30.2 for about 1,000 occupations.
